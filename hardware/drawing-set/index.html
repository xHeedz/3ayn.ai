General notes
  1. 01All dimensions in millimetres unless otherwise stated. Tolerance ±0.3 on moulded parts, ±0.10 on machined interfaces and optical seats.
  2. 02Third-angle projection throughout. Scales are stated per view; drawings are not to be measured from print.
  3. 03Enclosure outlines drawn solid; internal components and cavities drawn dashed. Section planes lettered A–A, B–B.
  4. 04Balloon numerals refer to the parts list carried on the same sheet.
  5. 05Frame front, temples and cane handle: injection-moulded PA12, matte bead-blast finish, no visible parting line on wearer-facing surfaces.
  6. 06Fasteners M1.4 × 3 A2 stainless, seated torque 0.08 N·m. Adhesive joints: methacrylate, 0.15 bond line.
  7. 07Ingress protection target IP54; silicone gasket at every enclosure seam and at both charge ports.
  8. 08Mass targets — glasses 48 g maximum including cells; cane 340 g maximum folded, balance point within 120 of the grip datum.
  9. 09Latency-critical sensing (pose estimation, extended-hand and approach detection) resolves on-device. No drawn signal path depends on a network round trip for narration.
  10. 10All audio output is bone-conducted. No transducer occludes the ear canal; ambient hearing is the wearer's primary channel and takes precedence.
  11. 11Every control is discoverable by touch alone: raised, spaced not less than 8 apart, and distinct in profile. No control relies on a printed legend.

Haptic vocabulary
EventPatternZone
Obstacle ahead, closingUpper
Drop-off or descending stepBoth
Person approaching (from glasses)Lower
Hand extended toward the wearerUpper
Pairing lostLower

Five patterns only, and none of them continuous. A cane that buzzes constantly is a cane that gets switched off; silence is the resting state and the wearer's own hearing is never competed with.

System interconnection

3AYN-SY-01
Split by latency requirement
Power

Glasses: 210 mAh, six hours of continuous sensing, charged by USB-C or by a four-way pogo dock the wearer can find by touch. Cane: 3400 mAh on the grip axis, thirty days of standby, charged from the same cable.

Pairing

Both devices join one event bus on the phone. Either one alone is useful; together the glasses see and the cane confirms, and no event is announced twice in two channels.

Consent

No frame leaves the body unless the wearer asks a question of it. Faces are matched only against people a caregiver enrolled knowingly; strangers are never stored, fingerprinted or clustered.
